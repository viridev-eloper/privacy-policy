Common questions

How do I download a chapter for offline listening?
Open the chapter you'd like to listen to and use the download option before you go offline — once downloaded, it will play without an internet connection.

How do I change the playback speed?
While a chapter is playing, use the speed control in the player to choose anywhere from 0.75x to 2x.

Does the app require an account?
No. The Catholic Audio Bible does not require you to sign up or log in to use any feature.
